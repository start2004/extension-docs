## Privacy Policy

**Extension Name:** Countdown Timer
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-03

### 1. Data We Collect

Countdown Timer collects **anonymous usage events** to improve the extension:

- **Interaction events**: which buttons you click and which pages you open within the extension (e.g., start, pause, cancel, repeat, open stats/options page), and outbound jumps such as opening the store or rating page
- **Timer presets**: which countdown duration you select (e.g., 600 seconds)
- **Language**: your browser UI locale (e.g., `en_US`)
- **Random identifier**: a randomly generated `client_id` (format `randomNumber.timestamp`), stored locally to distinguish installations

### 2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do **not** collect any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to: your name, email address, browsing history, web page content, or the countdown tasks you set.

### 3. How Data Is Used

- **No data is collected by default.** Data collection starts only after you explicitly agree via the analytics consent option in the extension's Options page.
- Data is sent to **Google Analytics** (via Measurement Protocol) and used solely for product improvement and usage analysis.
- Data is retained by Google Analytics according to its standard data retention settings, and is used only in aggregate form for the purposes above.
- We do **not** sell, rent, or share this data with any third party for any other purpose.
- All data is anonymous and cannot be linked back to you.

### 4. How to Opt Out

- Open the extension **Options** page and uncheck the analytics consent option to stop data collection immediately.
- Removing the extension also removes the local random identifier.
- You can prevent Google Analytics data collection with Google's opt-out tools: [https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the extension's release notes.

### 6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us via the support email listed on the extension's store page.

## 隐私政策

**插件名称：** Countdown Timer（倒计时）
**更新日期：** 2026-11-03

### 1. 我们收集的数据

Countdown Timer 收集**匿名的使用事件**，用于改进插件：

- **交互事件**：你在插件内点击了哪些按钮、打开了哪些页面（如开始、暂停、取消、重复、打开统计/设置页），以及跳转行为（如打开商店页、评价页）
- **时长档位**：你选择的倒计时时长（如 600 秒）
- **语言**：浏览器界面语言（如 `zh_CN`）
- **随机标识符**：随机生成的 `client_id`（格式：`随机整数.时间戳`），仅保存在本地，用于区分不同安装

### 2. 我们不收集的数据

我们不收集任何个人身份信息，包括但不限于：姓名、邮箱、浏览历史、网页内容，以及你设置的倒计时任务内容。

### 3. 数据用途

- **默认不收集任何数据。** 只有你在插件设置页明确勾选"同意统计"后，才会开始收集数据。
- 数据通过 **Google Analytics**（Measurement Protocol）上报，仅用于产品改进与使用分析。
- 数据由 Google Analytics 按其标准保留策略保存，且仅以聚合形式用于上述目的。
- 我们**不出售、不出租、不向任何第三方共享**这些数据用于其他目的。
- 所有数据均为匿名数据，无法关联到你个人。

### 4. 如何退出统计

- 打开插件**设置页**，取消勾选"同意统计"，立即停止数据上报。
- 卸载插件会同时删除本地存储的随机标识符。
- 你也可以使用 Google 官方退出工具阻止 Google Analytics 数据收集：[https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout)

### 5. 政策更新

我们可能更新本政策，重大变更会在插件更新说明中体现。

### 6. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请通过插件商店页面列出的支持邮箱联系我们。
